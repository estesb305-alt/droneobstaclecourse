Drone Obstacle Course

Fly through every gate in order, then land on the pad to finish. Your score is your time, plus penalties, minus bonuses, so the lowest total wins. Fly through the middle of a ring for a bonus: a bullseye takes 1 second off. A hard crash costs 5 seconds and sends you back to the last gate you cleared. Touching the water is a crash too, and so is landing too fast. Watch the wind arrow: it shows which way the wind is pushing you.
